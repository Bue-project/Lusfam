Our Story

Bridging Global Supply with Regional Demand

LusFam Energy Trading is the energy and commodities division of LusFam Trust Investments (Pvt) Ltd, a Zimbabwe-based investment company. We focus on facilitating the sourcing, supply coordination, and logistics support for refined petroleum products destined for Southern African markets.

Operating from Harare, we act as a regional market access partner for international trading houses — helping bridge global petroleum suppliers with emerging market demand across Southern Africa through transparent transactions and professional due diligence.
